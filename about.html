About AdCraft Blog

AdCraft Blog covers the craft of earning from content on small sites: where to place ad units, how to keep pages fast, and how to write in niches advertisers actually pay for. Everything runs on a free GitHub Pages deployment with no build step — the same architecture we recommend for anyone starting a monetized blog in 2026.

How we test

  • Real subscriptions, not trials on spec sheets. Tools are used with paid accounts and daily workloads; we publish actual costs in the currency we paid, including refunds and cancellations.
  • The same test file for every comparison. Meeting audio, real summaries, actual reply drafts — results are graded by rubric, not vibes, and the numbers go in the article.
  • Failures stay in the story. If a tool double-booked us or lost a draft, that's in the post. Negative results are the part most sites cut.
  • Refreshes are dated. Existing posts are re-tested on every major vendor release; a changelog line at the top says what changed.

How this site pays its hosting bill

Hosting is free (GitHub Pages). The pages carry display advertising — see the privacy & ads page for the third-party cookie disclosures. We do not run affiliate links or paid placements in reviews, so a tool's position in a comparison is never for sale.
